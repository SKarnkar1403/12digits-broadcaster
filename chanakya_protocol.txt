The Chanakya Protocol: Why AI Supremacy Belongs to the Indian Ocean.

Geopolitics is no longer just about land or blue-water navies; it is about the geography of compute. Artificial Intelligence requires three foundational pillars: raw silicon, massive energy grids, and high-velocity data pipelines. The Indian Ocean is the literal choke point for all three.

Underneath these waters lie the subsea fiber-optic cables that connect the western digital economy to eastern hardware manufacturing. Whoever controls the security, landing stations, and data flow through these maritime corridors controls the training latency of global AI networks. 

By applying classical Chanakya statecraft—focusing on strategic monopolies, defensive alliances, and internal self-reliance—the region is shifting from a transit route to a sovereign computing superpower. The future of intelligence isn't just built in the cloud; it is anchored in the deep ocean.
